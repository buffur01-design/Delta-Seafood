LMT Facility

Landingarmiðstøðin á Tvøroyri (LMT) is a modern fish landing, sorting and packaging facility located in Tvøroyri, Faroe Islands. It is designed to handle a wide range of fish species and products, ensuring high quality and efficiency in the processing and distribution of seafood. We have a new building which was complete in 2016. This has allowed us to expand operations and increase stream line efficiency. The facility is equipped with state-of-the-art technology and equipment to ensure that all fish products are handled with care and meet the highest standards of quality and safety. This in another step in our efforts to be an efficient and vertically integrated seafood company. We are proud to be able to offer our customers the best possible products and services, and we are committed to maintaining the highest standards of quality and sustainability in all aspects of our operations.

Today around 5 people are currently working at LMT, with a combination of these great employees and our state of the art equipment we are able to sort fish to any clients specifications. Whether it is size specifications, or packaging specifications.

In addition to fresh fish, we can supplement temporary shortages by thawing frozen fish.
