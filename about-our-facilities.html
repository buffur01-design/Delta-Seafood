About Us

Our three firms

We are a firm that operate in the fishing industry in the Faroe Islands. We have three firms: Delta Seafood, TG Seafood and LMT. All our firms are part of the same conglomerate. This allows us to have control of the entire value chain, from catching to processing and distribution. We are committed to sustainable fishing practices and high-quality seafood products done with the best fish products in the world.
