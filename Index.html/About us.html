About Delta Seafood

Delta Seafood is a seafood company specialising in wild-caught fish products from the Faroe Islands and international waters. We combine traditional craftsmanship with modern processing to deliver high-quality seafood to customers around the world.

Our team is dedicated to ensuring traceability and premium quality across every step of the value chain. We work closely with local fishing companies to ensure responsible sourcing and environmental stewardship.

Delta Seafood was founded in 1995 by people who already had experience in the seafood industry.

Back then it started in a 800 square meter facility in Tvøroyri. In the basement of Ísvirki. Today it has grown into a production facility with 4200 square meters of space.

There are around 45 people in full-time employment. Some of which have worked for us since day one.

Combining these very experienced professionals with modern technology allows us to maintain our high standards while continuously improving our processes.

As of today we are able to process around 80 tons of salted fish per week and 50 tons of frozen fish.

TG Seafood

TG Seafood is a point venuture between locals and Delta Seafood. TG Seafood specialises in the production of frozen products. TG Seafood was founded in 2005

Today around 65 people work for TG Seafood. With some having worked in the factory for many years.

TG Seafood has a production capacity of around 80 tons of frozen fish per week. With our dedicated team which allow us to produce high-quality frozen seafood.
